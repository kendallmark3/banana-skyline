About Banana Skyline

A small tribute to GORILLAS.BAS, the banana-throwing game that shipped with QBasic in the early 1990s and was, for a lot of us, the first program we ever opened up and changed.

Everything here is original — code, art and sound are generated in your browser. Nothing from the original game is included, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft or IBM.

Controls: sliders or − / + buttons; ← → angle, ↑ ↓ power (Shift for ×5), Space or Enter to throw, M sound, R retro mode. Add ?seed=123 to the address to replay a skyline.
